# Learners Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Learners Dashboard** is an interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze and visualize sales and business performance data. The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales, profit, orders, customers, employees, products, and yearly growth.

## 🎯 Key Insights

- Total Sales Amount and Total Profit Amount
- Overall Profit Margin and Orders Placed
- Year-over-Year (YOY) Growth Analysis
- Top Buying Customers
- Top-Selling Products
- Top Performing Employees
- Sales and Profit Analysis by Country
- Product Line-wise Sales Performance
- Monthly and Yearly Sales Trends
- Customer-wise Sales and Profit Analysis
- Credit Limit and Profit Margin Analysis

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI**
- **DAX**
- **Data Visualization**
- **Data Analysis**
- **Interactive Filters & Slicers**

## 📈 Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes interactive slicers that allow users to filter and analyze the data based on different categories. It combines KPI cards, bar charts, line charts, scatter plots, tables, and treemaps to provide meaningful business insights.

## 💡 Business Objective

The main objective of this project is to transform raw sales data into an interactive and easy-to-understand dashboard that helps identify sales trends, high-performing products and employees, profitable customers, and overall business performance.

## 📷 Dashboard Preview

The dashboard provides an executive-level overview of sales and performance metrics, making it easier for stakeholders to monitor business performance and make data-driven decisions.
